## Definition
How much of the **budget** has been achieved with actual **Net Sales**.

## Formula
`Budget Attainment = Net Sales / Budget` · YTD version: `Net Sales YTD / Budget YTD`

## Rules
- The budget is set per **month × region × category**.
- Read it from `${catalog}.semantic.sales_vs_budget_metrics` (`budget_attainment_pct`, `ytd_attainment_pct`).
- Above 100% = above budget.
