## Definition
Share of **Net Sales** that remains after the cost of goods sold.

## Formula
`Gross Margin % = SUM(margin) / SUM(net sales)`

## Rules
- It is a **ratio: never average it** across stores, months or categories. Always re-compute it from its components. The Metric View does this automatically (`gross_margin_pct`).
- Typical range in this business: 25–30%.
